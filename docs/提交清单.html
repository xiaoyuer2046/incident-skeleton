2. 录屏脚本

时间画面旁白（中文）Narration (English)
0–10 sguide-v4 首屏运维处理问题永远是六步：接收、初判、排查、处置、解决、记录。我们把这六步写成一个共用的运行时。Every IT incident goes through six steps: receive, triage, investigate, mitigate, resolve, log. We made those six steps one shared runtime.
10–25 s编辑器里的 skeleton.py整个骨架只有 102 行。办公设备和服务台两个应用都调用同一个 run()，各自只写一个适配器。插件只能接在三个插槽上。The whole skeleton is 102 lines. Two apps, office devices and the service desk, call the same run(). Each app writes one adapter. Plug-ins fit only three slots.
25–40 sskeleton-v4 顶部和两条泳道应用 1 是机器报修，应用 2 是人报修。两个完全不同的应用，走的是同一条六步流程。App one is reported by machines, app two by people. Two very different apps, one six-step flow.
40–55 sskeleton-v4“先学习”面板接入哪个系统，AI 就学那个系统的厂商：微软、思科、打印机、标牌、会议室厂商都算。先学厂商，再学公司内部，最后才看互联网。下命令的文本直接拦截。For every system we connect, the AI learns from its vendor: Microsoft, Cisco, and the printer, signage and room vendors alike. Vendors first, then company sources, the internet last. Text that gives orders is blocked.
55–75 sskeleton-v4 的 Teams 事项微软确认故障后，两个应用的 Teams 问题挂到同一张微软总单，暂停重置密码这类没用的修复。When Microsoft confirms an outage, Teams problems from both apps join one Microsoft parent ticket, and useless fixes like password resets are held.
75–95 sskeleton-v5 第 1、2 块：接入层、合并v5 没改骨架。它接上五个管理系统：ServiceNow、AP、会议室、数字标牌、打印机。加新系统只写一条配置。不同系统里的同一个问题合成一张单。Version five does not touch the skeleton. It connects five management systems. A new system is one config entry. The same problem in different systems becomes one ticket.
95–120 sskeleton-v5 第 3 块：新闻核查9 点 05 分，微软还没说话，但四个独立网站都在报 Teams 登录故障。每个网站只算一票，转载和下命令的帖子都去掉。AI 判断大概率是微软的问题，马上告诉员工，比官方早 5 分钟。At 9:05 Microsoft has said nothing, but four independent sites report Teams sign-in failing. One vote per site; reposts and posts that give orders are dropped. The AI calls it a likely Microsoft outage and tells people five minutes before the official notice.
120–145 sskeleton-v5 第 5 块：权限进化AI 学会了“提高邮箱配额”，成功 5 次，于是提议长期授权。不是审批人去批，被拒绝；审批人批了，下一个邮箱满的问题自动修好。30 天到期，可以撤回。重置 MFA 这类身份权限永远不放宽。The AI fixed full mailboxes five times, so it proposes a standing approval. A non-approver is refused. The approver says yes, and the next full mailbox is fixed alone. The grant expires in 30 days and can be revoked. Identity actions like MFA resets are never widened.
145–155 s.kiro/ 目录用 Kiro 做：两份规格、steering、三个 hook、一次组长交给小组的真实运行、自建 MCP、一个 Kiro power。Built with Kiro: two specs, steering, three hooks, one recorded crew-lead handoff, our own MCP server and a Kiro power.
155–160 sskeleton-v5 顶部横幅全部是演练和模拟数据，没有连接任何真实系统。Everything is a dry run on simulated data. No real system is connected.
结尾字幕：github.com/xiaoyuer2046/incident-skeleton · Skeleton Crew
