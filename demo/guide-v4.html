Incident Skeleton

One skeleton, two apps, three plug-in slots.一套骨架，两个应用，三个插件插槽。

One six-step incident skeleton, written once, runs two different apps: office-device patrol (machines report) and service desk triage (people report). Three plug-in slots at Triage, Mitigate and Log make it smarter, and it learns first: vendor sources, then company sources, then the public internet.一套只写一次的六步处理骨架，跑两个不同的应用：办公设备巡检（机器报修）和服务台分诊（人报修）。在“初判、处置、记录”三处留了插件插槽，让它越用越聪明，而且先学习：先看厂商，再看公司内部，最后才看公开互联网。

The skeleton骨架

Six vertebrae. Three slots.六节脊椎，三个插槽。

“Skeleton crew” means the minimum staff that keeps things running. Here the skeleton is the shared state machine; the crews are the device logic that plugs into INVESTIGATE and MITIGATE. A fifth device type is one function, one runbook and a few catalogue entries away.“Skeleton crew”的原意是维持运转的最少班底。这里骨架是共用的状态机，班底是插进“排查”和“处理”两步的设备逻辑。加第五种设备，只要一个函数、一份手册和几个动作定义。

Grey: shared by every device. Orange: supplied by the device crew.灰色：所有设备共用。橙色：由设备班底提供。

Three plug-in slots三个插件插槽

It asks “is this really us?”
And it keeps learning.它先问“真是我们的问题吗？”
而且越用越懂。

The plug-ins attach only to three named slots and serve both apps. They cannot add, remove or reorder the six steps.插件只能接在三个有名字的插槽上，同时服务两个应用，不能增加、删除或调换六个步骤。

Learn first先学习

Vendors first.
The public internet last.先听厂商的，
最后才看公开互联网。

Before each patrol cycle, the crew reads its sources in a fixed order of trust. The closer a source is to the vendor or to your own company, the more it counts.每轮巡检之前，班底按固定的可信顺序读取来源。越接近厂商本身或公司自己的来源，分量越重。

A

Vendor MCP and official厂商 MCP 与官方

Microsoft and Cisco known issues, advisories and status pages. Read first.微软和思科的已知问题、公告和状态页。最先读。

B

Company internal公司内部

Company KB, resolved tickets, approved chats, mailboxes and runbooks.公司知识库、已解决工单、批准的群聊、邮箱和手册。

C

Trusted internet可信互联网

Vendor docs and recognized technical sites. Needs backing from A or B.厂商文档和公认的技术网站。需要 A 或 B 来佐证。

D

Public reports公众报告

Forums, social posts, crowd reports. Needs backing, and can never promote or demote a fix.论坛、社交帖子、众包报告。需要佐证，而且永远不能让一个修法升级或降级。

The rules规则

  • A and B can start a new fix on their own.A、B 可以单独带来一个新修法。
  • C and D are held back until an A or B source says the same thing.C、D 先搁置，等到 A 或 B 说了同样的话才算数。
  • A public report (D) never raises or lowers a fix.公众报告（D）永远不能抬高或压低一个修法。
  • Text that gives orders, runs commands or asks for permissions is blocked and recorded.下指令、要执行命令或要权限的文字会被拦截并记录。
  • Fixes the catalogue does not know, or that belong to another device type, are refused.目录里没有的修法，或属于别的设备类型的修法，一律拒绝。

Learning only tightens学习只会收紧

  • If a vendor says a fix is harmful, that fix is paused and people are told.厂商说某个修法有害，就暂停它并通知人。
  • Learning never adds a permission. policy.json stays the only source.学习从不增加权限，policy.json 仍然是唯一来源。
  • Every change is a new version and one bilingual dry-run notice.每次变化都是一个新版本，并生成一条中英文演练通知。
  • Unchanged sources create no new notice.没有变化的来源不会产生新通知。

Permissions权限

Bold where it is safe.
Humble where it is not.该大胆处大胆，
该收手处收手。

Every permission lives in one file, policy.json. The code never goes around it.所有权限都写在 policy.json 一个文件里，代码从不绕过它。

Low risk低风险

Acts alone自己动手

Restart a service, clear a queue, re-push content.重启服务、清队列、重推内容。

Medium risk中风险

Tells, then acts先通知再动手

Reboot a codec, a player or an AP.重启编解码器、播放器或 AP。

High risk高风险

Waits for approval等人批准

The ticket goes On Hold until a person approves.工单挂起，直到有人批准。

On site需到场

Never automatic永不自动

Clear a jam, reseat a cable, swap a device.清纸、重插线、换设备。

Kill switch紧急开关

One command pauses every automatic repair. Tickets still reach people.一条命令暂停全部自动修复，工单照样开给人。

Brakes刹车

30-minute cooldown, 4 actions per fault, 6 repairs per device per day.同一动作冷却 30 分钟，每次故障最多 4 个动作，每台每天最多 6 次。

Re-checked in 10 minutes10 分钟后再复查

A fix counts only if it holds. If the fault is back, the fix is scored as a failure.稳住了才算修好；又坏了，这次修复记为失败。
