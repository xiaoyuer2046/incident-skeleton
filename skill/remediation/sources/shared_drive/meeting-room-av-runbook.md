## Panel frozen, room cannot join
If the touch panel is frozen and the room cannot join, reboot the codec first. Restarting the room app rarely helps on Poly X50.

If the codec reboot does not help, reset the room controller profile from the admin portal. Firmware 2.3 corrupts it after a power blip.

## No audio in the room
Check the DSP mute state in the Q-SYS designer and reseat the USB audio cable.
